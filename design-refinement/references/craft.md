# Craft checklist

**Type:** few sizes, clear steps (page title > section title > body > meta). Weight and colour carry hierarchy, not size alone. Sentence case. Numbers right-aligned and tabular where they are compared.

**Shape:** one radius scale (larger for panels, smaller for controls). Light borders, few shadows, few nested boxes, no dividers that do not separate anything.

**Icons and chrome:** one icon set and stroke weight. Remove decorative icons. Show secondary controls (help, actions) on hover and focus, and always on touch. Tooltips must not cover the value they explain.

**Colour:** colour carries meaning, not decoration. Keep numbers neutral and show direction with a small coloured mark. Neutral states are gray. One ramp per measure with distinct steps. In dark mode, check muted text contrast, chart lines and fills, and that each ramp step stays distinct. Review dark mode on its own.

**Charts:** small, readable ticks, a quiet grid, the first and last labels kept at every width, the same bar width across related charts, labels in full before you truncate.

**Tables:** comfortable row height, headers aligned with their values, inline bars in the column they measure, empty columns hidden, plain values ("Unknown", names, not codes).

**States:** no fake data in empty states. Use an icon, one sentence, and an optional action. Errors are visibly errors, and their copy does not repeat the button. Cards fit their content.

**Small screens:** complex charts become lists, tabs become a select, wide tables scroll with an edge fade or drop low-value columns, filters wrap without orphans, grids stay even.

**Copy:** short, plain, the same noun for the same thing. Detail goes in a drawer or tooltip, not on the card face.
